Use this session for retrieval and explanation rather than another broad reading assignment. State assumptions, trace a concrete mechanism, show one failure case, and explain the evidence behind a decision.

Spend roughly two minutes tracing a request through browser, server, identity, permission, cache and data. Then let the interviewer change one fact: a different user, stale value, failed dependency or second instance. Explain the next observation that distinguishes possible causes. Ask a clarifying question when the requirement is ambiguous.

The small Python drill deliberately mishandles accumulation across multiple paid rows. Predict the failing result, repair your own copy, run its checks and add one changed case. Explain the invariant and limitations instead of merely showing a green result.

Be precise about experience. “I ran this local exercise with a prepared environment” and “I operated this system in production” are different claims. The app stores practice and confidence but does not judge the correctness or clarity of your free explanation. Use a tutor or peer for follow-up challenges. One employer’s interview guidance is not a forecast of every interview.

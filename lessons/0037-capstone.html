Finish with a verified slice, not a new full-stack build. Choose an existing mechanism from the course: scoped private reads, a replay-safe import, measured memory behaviour, a container lifecycle, a tested dbt model, or an Airflow retry.

Reuse the prepared code, predict one relevant result, execute it, and change one meaningful edge case. Rerun the appropriate checks and explain why they support the intended behaviour. Keep this narrow enough for 15 minutes of practical work after recall and setup.

The bundled data_lab capstone combines a replayed SQLite import with owner-scoped warm cache reads and anonymous rejection. It is an in-process synthetic service slice, not a deployed production API. Its existing assertions are agent-authored scaffolding. Your contribution is the changed case, your actual commands/results and your own explanation.

Use the repository’s exercise template for a public write-up: requirement, mechanism, what changed, commands/results, assistance, limitations and next check. Do not publish private assessments or employer data. The browser rubric checks sample record fields, not authenticity or understanding. Have a tutor review your explanation and revisit a changed task later before claiming retained competence.
